# Word list source

`american-english.txt` is the Debian `wamerican` package, version
2020.12.07-2 (SCOWL size 50, American spelling), fetched with
`apt-get download wamerican` and unpacked from
`/usr/share/dict/american-english`. License and credits: `COPYRIGHT`.

smart-solve's candidate words: the words that fit a partly revealed board
word. It is combined with the distinct words of `train.csv` and `dev.csv`,
which cover names the list lacks.
